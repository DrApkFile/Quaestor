## C: D5 — holdings, not complete reconciliation

Each book has a signed opening {v,book,openingBalance per asset,at}, anchored by a new Books v2 event at registration. Signed close balances = previous close/opening + IN - OUT - cash-out fees, per asset. Close includes Gloam funds proof (max 4 notes; consolidate to self if needed), threshold equal to closing balance, context bound to quaestor|holdings|book|period.

HOLDINGS_COVER_BOOKS requires a valid proof, full-history root membership and every nonzero revealed nullifier unspent at the close block, pinned. HOLDINGS_SHORT means the books claim more than can be proven; UNKNOWN otherwise. Never show adds up. C6 claim exactly: “the business holds at least the balance its books imply; does not detect income kept off the books or payments made from unbooked funds”.
