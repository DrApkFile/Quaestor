# M3 brief — sealed books, period grants and Books.sol

Builder authorization follows the M2 anonymous-ticket must-fix and the due-by-M3 lint/cache fixes. Read PRD sections 5–7 and 10. Do not implement M4 verdicts.

## Integration spikes first

- S09: pinned eth_call of business pqcKeyStatus at 0x0A02; require registered, algorithm 1, possessionProven.
- S16: raw ML-DSA-87 signature over a 32-byte message; pqcVerify at 0x0A01 accepts it and rejects a flipped signature. Record the expected context from official QoreChain source/docs.
- If inactive, record DECISIONS D3 and the PRD fallback (signature stored; verification off chain).

## Pure packages/core

No network or clock. Exact dependencies. Time and random key bytes are arguments.

- Period: book plus UTC YYYY-MM-DD periodId; demo-scale 1-day periods (D10). EpochKey ordinal starts at 1, key 32 random bytes.
- Canonical entry: v, book, period, index, prevEntrySha3, direction, asset, amount, paymentCommitment, counterparty {address,name}, purpose, category, notes, receipt.
- sealBatch with period key, context quaestor:<book>:entry and batchIndex index. Hybrid QoreSign signature of SHA3-256(blob), using SDK business keys.
- Real receipt witness/proof from local receipt circuit, exact amount, reveal 1, context bound to quaestor|book|period|index. Verification of Groth16 is M4.
- Grant: encodeEpochKey, hybrid anonymous seal to verified auditor registry/card, purpose quaestor:<book>:period-key, AAD book|period|auditor qor1. Signed grant record {v,book,period,auditor,envelopeSha3,issuedAt}. Business self-copy too.
- Signed close record {v,book,period,entryCount,lastEntrySha3}. No reconciliation fields before D5.
- Test granted/refused periods, outsider grant refusal, edited blob hash/signature, removed/reordered entry chains and receipt context binding.

## Books.sol

Owner-only custom errors/events. registerBook checks registered algorithm-1 possession-proven PQ key. anchorEntry enforces nextIndex/prevSha3 and open period. anchorGrant records auditor grant hash. closePeriod verifies raw PQ signature of keccak256(abi.encodePacked("quaestor:close", chainid, contract, book, period, closeSha3)), or declared D3 fallback; locks period. Foundry tests mock precompiles with vm.etch/vm.mockCall. Deploy and record receipt/address.

## pnpm evidence --m3

- Business registers book and anchors sealed/signed entries for M2's 2-QOR payment, M2b's 1-QOR payment and an incoming client-to-business 1-QOR anonymous-ticket payment.
- Close demo period A, grant A to auditor. Period B has at least one entry and only a business self-copy of its key.
- Fresh auditor process loads only auditor keys, opens A, decrypts every A entry, checks blob hashes against anchors and every signature, and is refused B with NOT_GRANTED.
- evidence/m3-books.json: observed hashes, book ID, per-entry ciphertext hashes and anchor receipts, grants/close, auditor results. No plaintext entries, keys or note secrets. C2 PROVEN only on full success.
